Before You Begin

Run through your first year. Tap anywhere on the screen (or press SPACE) to hop obstacles. At each glowing flag, the run pauses for a real first-year dilemma.

Watch your lantern: it flares bright when you use a resilience skill, dims sharply when you miss one, and flickers low each time you stumble. Six scenarios are drawn from a pool of sixteen — every run is different.
